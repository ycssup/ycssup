05 · SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Sources, calculation conventions & validation

The dashboard separates the current 14 Sep market snapshot from completed EOD chart history, and distinguishes market-series sources from official benchmark/reference sources and news used for interpretation.

Calculation & cutoff

  • Snapshot cutoff: approximately 19:30 HKT on 14 Sep 2026. CSI 300 and China 10Y use the completed 14 Sep Asian session; FX, commodities, US 10Y and crypto use the latest available quote around the snapshot time.
  • US equities: the S&P 500 benchmark remains at its latest completed cash close, 11 Sep 2026; Monday futures are used only in commentary and are not substituted for the cash index level.
  • Performance: 1D / 1W readings are refreshed to the latest observation. 3M / 6M / 1Y use the same historical reference dates as v17 for comparability. Bond changes are shown in basis points.
  • Detailed line charts: retain completed 1M EOD history through 11 Sep; today’s refreshed level is shown in each benchmark strip above the chart.

Benchmark selection

  • Displayed market snapshot: Investing.com historical / real-time instrument pages for CSI 300, USD/CNH, EUR/USD, XAU/USD, Brent, US 10Y, China 10Y, BTC/USD and ETH/USD; S&P 500 latest cash close cross-checked with Reuters.
  • Market interpretation: Reuters reporting dated 14 Sep 2026 for oil / shipping developments, equity-futures risk tone, dollar and gold moves, China bond-market context and central-bank expectations.
  • OTC FX, spot metals and 24/7 crypto can differ slightly across vendors because of timestamp and close conventions; this dashboard is for class analysis, not trading execution.

Primary series & validation

  • Two representative benchmarks per asset class balance global relevance with China / Asia relevance where appropriate.
  • S&P 500 / CSI 300: US and mainland-China equity risk; USD/CNH / EUR/USD: China-related FX conditions plus a major developed-market USD pair.
  • Gold / Brent: safe-haven / real-rate dynamics and energy-inflation transmission; US 10Y / China 10Y: policy and growth divergence; BTC / ETH: large-cap crypto risk.
  • Official reference validation: Federal Reserve / U.S. Treasury, ChinaBond / CCDC, S&P DJI, China Securities Index, ICE, LBMA, ECB and CoinGecko where applicable.

AI use, verification & limitations

  • AI use: assisted with dashboard structure, HTML/CSS/SVG coding, wording, calculation QA and cross-asset synthesis. AI was not treated as the source of record for market data.
  • Verification: current levels and time-sensitive market claims were cross-checked against the cited market / official / Reuters sources before display.
  • Known limitation: this is a mixed-market-time snapshot, not a synchronized global close. The dashboard explicitly keeps the S&P 500 at its latest cash close and separates today’s snapshot from completed EOD line-chart history.
